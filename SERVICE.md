# Service scope

## Eligible work

The initial service covers public Python AI and open-source projects. A review follows the source revision, pages, Python version, operating system, and examples agreed with the requester.

Checks can cover installation instructions, environment configuration, imports, documented parameter names, and examples that can run without paid model calls. The report distinguishes source inspection from executed tests.

The pilot costs USD 25 and covers up to one hour. An optional monthly service costs USD 79 and covers up to two hours. These are the offered prices, not estimates of a client's savings or promises of future results.

## Delivery and acceptance

Before work starts, both parties agree on the pages, tests, time limit, delivery date, and acceptance conditions in writing. A review request alone is not a contract or authorization to charge.

Delivery includes a Markdown findings report and the commands and observed results for the checks performed. Where the scope includes a correction, delivery also includes a patch or a pull request. Upstream review and merge decisions remain with the project maintainer.

Each correction covers one issue and at most 100 changed lines. One revision per correction is included within the overall time limit. Additional work requires a new agreement. A report may find no defect; the service pays for the agreed review, not a guaranteed bug count.

## Monthly service

Monthly work starts only after a pilot is accepted and the monthly scope is agreed. It includes two reviews of the same agreed pages, a change report, and up to two small corrections within two hours total.

Future months are optional. There is no automatic payment collection. Either party can decline the next period before its work starts. Payment remains due only for work ordered and delivered under the written agreement.

## Limits

The service does not include production access, application development, security testing, paid API use, private repositories, or emergency support. It does not certify that all documentation is correct or that an application is secure. Checks outside the agreed environment are identified as unverified.

AI assists the work. Executed tests and source references support the findings. Clients should review proposed changes before production use.

## Information and payment

Public requests must contain public project information only. Do not send passwords, tokens, personal data, employer files, or customer data.

After scope agreement, billing details are exchanged through an agreed private channel. A PayPal invoice applies only to agreed work. There is no request to send money as a gift or to pay for unapproved work.
